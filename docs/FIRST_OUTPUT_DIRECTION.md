Scope: prompt/instruction refinement only, not a new runtime schema, renderer or quality classifier. Current installation and released archives remain unchanged.

## Evidence and decision replay

A same-source interior original and its accepted V1 were inspected in the authorized task. V1 used global native adjustments followed by a narrow vertical crop. Technical identity and texture protection passed, but treating the large warm wall and furniture as distractions removed part of the interior/exterior story. The new direction must identify the value of warm negative space, furniture, cool window light and foreground-to-background relationships before choosing a crop. A tighter subject is insufficient evidence of the complete photographic goal. This reassessment does not change the historical acceptance ledger.

Existing same-source coast V1/V2 records identify a water/pier separation issue already foreseeable in V1. V2 used a real water mask, kept the composition, and darkened water to distinguish the illuminated pier. The new initial direction must consider that relationship before choosing whole-image shadow lifting; execute a local step only if needed and if the actual mask is sound. This is not a rule to darken all water or apply this scene's palette elsewhere.

These are retrospective identification checks, not a controlled new-photo quality experiment. No historical photograph is edited, no old record is rewritten, and no new image service call is made by this change.
